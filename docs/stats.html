By class

RailRadar's own classification, not ours, and always the whole fleet — the filters below don't reach it. Figures are pooled across every journey in the class rather than averaged over its trains, so a service seen once doesn't count as loudly as one seen weekly. AT ARRIVAL is the share of those journeys read within 6 h of the arrival they describe: for services scheduled longer than a day the live endpoint usually returns the run that departed most recently rather than the one arriving, so their delays describe a train still mid-journey. A low figure means read that row as how late partway, not how late it arrived.

All trains

Every figure covers all journeys on record, not a fixed window, so a service tracked for three days and one tracked for three weeks sit in the same table — read RUNS before comparing them. A journey counts only once a poll caught it actually running and returned a delay; an asterisk means further journeys were seen but carried no usable reading. DELAY % is the average delay as a share of the scheduled journey time; it is the only column that compares a Shatabdi to a thirty-hour long-haul fairly.
