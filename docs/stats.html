All trains

Every figure covers all journeys on record, not a fixed window, so a service tracked for three days and one tracked for three weeks sit in the same table — read RUNS before comparing them. A journey counts only once a poll caught it actually running and returned a delay; an asterisk means further journeys were seen but carried no usable reading. DELAY % is the average delay as a share of the scheduled journey time; it is the only column that compares a Shatabdi to a thirty-hour long-haul fairly.
